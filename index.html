Spreadsheet format & privacy

Use exactly three columns: BITS ID, Course, Total Marks. Keep student IDs as text to preserve leading zeros. Exclude students receiving NC. Fractional marks are rejected; apply your course’s rounding policy before uploading. Formula cells are not accepted.

Only the first worksheet is read. Files are processed locally and are not stored after you close or refresh this page. A valid replacement workbook resets grading decisions only in this institute workspace.
